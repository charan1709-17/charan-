About

I'm Charan, a 3D and motion designer passionate about turning ideas into engaging visual experiences. I work with tools like Blender, Unreal Engine, and After Effects to create 3D visuals, animations, and motion graphics. I'm especially excited by cinematic visuals, creative storytelling, and projects that blend design, technology, and imagination.

The process

From first reference to final render.

01

Concept

Moodboards, references, and rough blocking to lock the mood, camera, and story before any final assets are built.

02

Look development

Modeling, texturing, and lighting come together in Blender or Unreal until the scene reads exactly as intended.

03

Animation

Camera moves, simulations, and motion are refined pass by pass, keeping performance and story front and center.

04

Render & composite

Final render passes are composited and graded in After Effects for a finished, cinematic result.
