About

I'm Charan, a 3D and motion designer who likes turning half-formed ideas into things that actually move. I split my time between Blender, Unreal Engine and After Effects — building 3D visuals, animation and motion graphics that lean cinematic.

What pulls me in is storytelling: lighting a scene until it feels like a place, timing a shot until it feels inevitable, syncing motion until it feels alive. I'm most excited by work that sits at the edge of design, technology and imagination — the projects where the brief is really "make me believe this."
