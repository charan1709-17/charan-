About Me

I'm Charan, a 3D and motion designer turning ideas into visual experiences worth pausing for.

I work mainly in Blender and Unreal Engine to build 3D visuals and environments, then bring them to life in After Effects — animation, motion graphics, and everything in between.

I'm especially excited by cinematic visuals, creative storytelling, and projects that sit at the crossroads of design, technology and imagination.

My mission: make every frame worth pausing for.
